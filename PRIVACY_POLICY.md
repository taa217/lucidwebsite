# Privacy Policy for Lucid AI

**Last Updated:** August 1, 2026

Welcome to **Lucid AI** ("Lucid", "we", "us", or "our"), accessible via [https://lucid-ai.co](https://lucid-ai.co) and [https://app.lucid-ai.co](https://app.lucid-ai.co).

We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use our web application, tools, APIs, and services (collectively, the "Services").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account & Profile Information:** When you sign up or log in (via WorkOS or Google Sign-In), we collect your name, email address, profile picture, and authentication identifiers.
* **User Content & Study Materials:** Documents, notes, text prompts, assignment materials, course files, and audio/speech input that you upload or submit to the platform.
* **Customer Support & Communications:** Feedback, support tickets, and communication history when you reach out to us.

### B. Information Authorized via Google Integrations (Google OAuth & Google Classroom)
When you connect your Google Account or Google Classroom to Lucid AI, with your explicit authorization, we access and process:
* **User Identity:** Primary Google account email address and basic profile information.
* **Google Classroom Data:** Enrolled courses, assignment titles, instructions, due dates, course material metadata, and submission statuses (`classroom.courses.readonly`, `classroom.coursework.me`, `classroom.student-submissions.me.readonly`).
* **Google Drive Files:** Access to files you select or create for coursework attachment and turn-in (`drive.file`, `drive.readonly`).

### C. Automatically Collected Information
* **Usage & Device Data:** IP address, browser type, operating system, device characteristics, pages visited, time spent, and feature interactions.
* **Analytics Data:** Collected via privacy-centric analytics (e.g., PostHog) to understand product performance and optimize user experience.

### D. Billing & Subscription Data
* Payments are processed securely through our third-party payment processor, **Dodo Payments**. Lucid AI does not store raw credit card or bank account details on our servers. We receive payment confirmation status, subscription plan tier, billing renewal dates, and transaction IDs.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following specific purposes:
* **To Provide & Personalize Services:** Enabling AI-assisted study features, document summarization, voice/TTS audio synthesis, interactive learning, and assignment organization.
* **To Synchronize Google Classroom & Drive:** Allowing seamless retrieval of assignment details and automated submission/turn-in of completed deliverables to your Google Classroom courses.
* **To Manage Accounts & Subscriptions:** Authentication, passwordless login, subscription tier management, usage quota tracking, and customer service.
* **To Improve Our Services:** Analyzing usage patterns, monitoring service performance, fixing software bugs, and enhancing platform security.
* **To Communicate:** Sending operational updates, subscription receipts, security alerts, and customer support responses.

---

## 3. Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use Disclosure

Lucid AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the **[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy)**, including the **Limited Use** requirements.

Specifically:
1. **Purpose Limitation:** We only use Google OAuth data to provide user-facing features that are prominent in the request interface (e.g., viewing assignment tasks and attaching completed deliverables).
2. **No Data Transfers to Third Parties:** We do NOT transfer Google user data to third parties, except as necessary to provide or improve user-facing features, comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger/acquisition.
3. **No Human Inspection:** Humans do NOT read your Google user data unless:
   - You provide explicit consent to inspect specific data for troubleshooting;
   - It is necessary for security purposes (e.g., investigating abuse or security incidents);
   - It is required by applicable legal process.
4. **No AI Model Training on Private Google Data:** We do NOT use raw Google user data obtained via Google Workspace/Classroom APIs to train generalized, un-aggregated Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Machine Learning (ML) models.

---

## 4. How We Share & Disclose Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, strictly bound by data protection obligations:

* **Authentication Providers:** WorkOS & Google OAuth (for secure user sign-in).
* **Cloud Infrastructure & AI APIs:** Google Cloud Platform (Cloud Run, Cloud Storage, Firestore, Gemini AI APIs) for hosting, processing, and generating educational insights.
* **Payment Processors:** Dodo Payments (for payment transaction handling).
* **Product Analytics:** PostHog (for aggregated usage monitoring and service optimization).
* **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of Lucid AI, our users, or the public.

---

## 5. Data Security & Retention

### A. Security Measures
We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration, including:
* HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit.
* AES-256 encryption for stored files and database items at rest.
* Strict IAM (Identity & Access Management) policies enforcing minimum privilege access.

### B. Retention Policy
* Account details and user content are stored as long as your account remains active.
* Text-to-speech audio caches and transient document processing files are automatically pruned according to automated retention schedules.
* If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently removed from our active databases within 30 days, except where legal compliance requires longer retention.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:
* **Access & Export:** You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
* **Correction:** You can update or correct inaccurate account information directly in your Account Settings.
* **Deletion:** You may request account deletion at any time by contacting support.
* **Revoke Google Permissions:** You can revoke Lucid AI's access to your Google Account at any time via your [Google Security Account Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
